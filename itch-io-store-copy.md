# Harvest Hollow — itch.io Copy

## Title / Tagline

**Harvest Hollow**
*A tiny farm, grown together. Grab a friend — this one's two-player.*

## Short Pitch

Harvest Hollow is a free, browser-based co-op farm-sim built for exactly one thing: playing with a friend. Open the game, share the link it gives you, and watch a second farmer land right next to you on the same shared plot. Till, plant, water, and harvest together, split a shared wallet, and spend your gold on plot upgrades and cosmetic decorations as your little farm grows. No downloads, no accounts — just a link and a friend.

**This is co-op only — you need a second player.** Opening the link and playing solo won't do much; the farm is built for two (up to six, if you've got a crew). Send the invite link to whoever you're playing with before you start planting.

One more heads-up: the server sleeps when nobody's around, so the very first load can take 30-60 seconds to spin up. That's normal, not broken — hang tight and your farm will load right in.

## Feature Bullets

- **Real co-op, not co-located solo play** — share one invite link and land on the same 6x6 plot, live, with up to 6 players.
- **A farm you actually run together** — till, plant, water, and harvest as a team, with a shared wallet everyone spends from.
- **Watering matters** — leave a planted tile dry too long and it wilts. Keep an eye on your crops, not just your friend's.
- **Grow your plot, upgrade your tools** — spend gold earned together to expand the farm and work faster.
- **Decorate your hollow** — fences, a well, a windmill — cosmetic extras once the basics are humming.
- **See your friends** — animated player avatars so you always know who's doing what, where.
- **No install, plays anywhere** — runs in the browser, mobile-friendly with touch controls.
- **Your farm saves itself** — progress persists on the server and picks up right where you left off, every time you reopen the link.
- **Built-in onboarding** — a first-visit guide and a gentle hint nudge you toward your first action, so nobody's staring at an empty field wondering what to do.

---

**Read before playing:** Harvest Hollow requires a second player. Open the link, copy your invite URL, and send it to a friend — the farm won't do much with just one of you in it. First load after idle may take up to a minute while the free server wakes up.
