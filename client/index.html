Welcome to Harvest Hollow

  1. Till, Plant, Water, Harvest — pick a tool up top, then tap a tile. Cycle through all four to grow a crop.
  2. Sell All turns harvested wheat into gold, shared with everyone on this farm.
  3. Spend gold on Expand Plot and Upgrade Tool as they unlock — more space, and tools that act on multiple tiles at once.
  4. This farm is shared. Use Invite to copy the link and bring up to 5 friends onto the same field — everyone can till, plant, water, or harvest any tile.
